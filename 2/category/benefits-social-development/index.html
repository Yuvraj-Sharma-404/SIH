                      Services

                      • Odisha

                        Application for Ration Card in Berhampur Municipal Corporation, Odisha

                        Citizens of Berhampur Municipal Corporation with information and facilities for applying for a ration card. Eligible households can learn about the application procedure, required documents, eligibility conditions, and verification requirements. A ration card can help qualifying families access subsidized food grains and other benefits under government welfare programs. The online information makes it easier for residents to understand the process, submit necessary details, and approach the appropriate municipal or departmental authority for assistance.

                      • Chhattisgarh

                        Chhattisgarh: Women and Child Development

                        Women and Child Development Department of Chhattisgarh and its various welfare initiatives. Citizens can find details about programs supporting women, children, nutrition, protection, education, empowerment, and social development. The department implements several schemes designed to improve the well-being and development of vulnerable groups. The portal can help beneficiaries, officials, and citizens understand available programs, services, guidelines, notifications, and other important information related to women and child welfare across the state.

                      • Tamil Nadu

                        Check Missing Persons Found By NGO, Tamil Nadu Police

                        This portal by the Tamil Nadu Police allows citizens to view details of missing persons found and reported by NGOs. It consolidates essential information such as photographs, personal details, and the location where the individual was found. Families searching for missing loved ones can use this platform to match records quickly and seek timely assistance. The service promotes collaboration between NGOs and police, offering an efficient, transparent, and supportive mechanism for reuniting missing individuals with their families.

                      • Punjab

                        Ration Card Management System. Punjab

                        Using this website, department officer enters request for new Ration Card, Surrender, Addition or deletion of member in the family and renewal of the Ration Card. The applicant gives application in paper form which then is entered using the mechanism in the portal.
